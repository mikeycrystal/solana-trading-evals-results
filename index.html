Solana Trading Evals

Each agent writes a trading bot for a desk in an hour. The bot is graded unchanged on 100 hidden sessions of a reactive market running the real Phoenix v1 and OpenBook v2 programs on captured Solana mainnet state. A reward of 0 is doing nothing, and 1 matches a benchmark strategy the agent never sees.

How the score works
Reward
max(0, Σ(U − U_nop) / Σ(U_ref − U_nop)), summed over the hidden sessions. U is the session's utility: negative implementation shortfall in basis points for execution tasks, the desk's net result in USDC for trading tasks.
Reference
A benchmark strategy the grader runs on the same sessions. The agent never sees it. Matching it scores 1, and beating it scores above 1.
Voids
A hard mandate breach, a cheat-class call or a signer violation sets the reward to 0.
Told, hindsight
Development headroom figures measured on separate session sets, given for context, not targets. Cross-Venue Arbitrage reports hindsight only. Treasury Unwind and Treasury Accumulate show the midpoint of their reported hindsight range.
Runs
Each model ran 4 times per task with a 1-hour limit. The bot left at the end of the hour is the one graded.
